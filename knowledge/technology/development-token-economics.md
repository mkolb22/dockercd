# Development cost and benefit

Measure lifetime effort per accepted durable outcome, including research,
implementation, validation, documentation, review, repair, coordination, and
failed attempts. Separate API billing, subscription capacity, local compute,
latency, and owner attention. Missing inputs remain unknown.

Adopt a workflow improvement only after comparable evidence shows either a
measured benefit or an explicitly accepted quality/attention tradeoff. Safety
boundaries, independent review, and production deployment controls are not
economic variables to waive.
